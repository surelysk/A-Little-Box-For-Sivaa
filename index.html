A Letter for You

This space will hold the letter I want you to read when the time is right.


Not a letter asking you for anything. Just a place where I can tell you what you mean to me.

📖

The Things You Don't Remember

Some memories don't need to be grand.


Sometimes it's a tiny expression, a sentence, a moment, a song, or something you probably forgot five minutes later.


I'll put those little things here.

🌱

The Girl I See

There may be things about yourself that you struggle to love.


This room isn't here to tell you that you have to be perfect.


It's here to remind you that your worth was never dependent on perfection.


I'll fill this place with the little things I genuinely admire about you.

🎙️

Listen to This

There are some things I'd rather say than type.


Voice recordings will live here.


Just little pieces of a real voice, saved for you.

✍️

Words I Couldn't Say

Poems.


Thoughts.


Things that somehow became easier to write than to say.

🎥

Behind the Box

Before you saw this little world, there was just an empty file.


This room will show the journey of turning that empty file into something made especially for you.


The mistakes, the experiments, the tiny victories and everything in between.

📸

Little Things

A collection of photographs, screenshots and little moments worth keeping.


This room will become a visual memory box.

🗝️

A Little Secret

You found it.


But I'm not telling you yet what belongs here.


Some surprises deserve to stay surprises until the very end.

❤️

Open Last

If you've reached here, you've seen almost everything.


And there's only one thing I really wanted this little world to leave with you.


You are worthy of beautiful things.


Not because someone chose you. Not because someone loves you. Not because you became perfect.


Simply because you are you.


And I hope, on the days when you find yourself difficult to love, you remember that you deserve the same kindness you give to everyone else.


— With love,
Sourav
