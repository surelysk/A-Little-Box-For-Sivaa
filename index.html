Open Last

If you've reached here, you've seen almost everything.


But I hope this little world leaves you with something more important than anything inside it.


You are worthy of beautiful things.


Not because someone chose you. Not because someone loves you. Not because you became perfect.


Simply because you are you.


And on the days when you find yourself difficult to love, I hope you remember to be gentle with yourself.
